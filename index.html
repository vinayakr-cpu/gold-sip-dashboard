Verdict details

  1. One-year lows

    Spot gold traded near $3,530-3,586/oz, about 31.5% below January's $5,602/oz record, and MCX December futures closed at INR 1,23,150 / 10g (-INR 4,302, -3.4%), the lowest since August 2025 - today's INR 11,510 / gram (22K) is the cheapest 22K print in over a year.

  2. Favourable deltas

    Today is INR 470 / gram (-3.9%) cheaper than yesterday's INR 11,980 / gram, INR 1,305 / gram (-10.2%) cheaper than 13 Nov and INR 2,060 / gram (-15.2%) cheaper than 21 Oct - every reference window is green for a buyer.

  3. Bounce risk

    IBJA President Prithviraj Kothari sees RSI below 20 and a likely bounce to $3,630-3,680 (about INR 1,25,000-1,27,000 / 10g), which would add roughly INR 150-350 / gram to 22K - more than the ~INR 130 / gram point saving from waiting until 25 Nov.

  4. Hike priced in

    Markets put ~95.7% odds on a December Fed hike with another partly priced for January; after a ~15% nine-session MCX slide, hawkishness is largely already in the price, limiting fresh downside per session.

  5. Wedding floor

    The World Gold Council expects lower prices plus the ongoing wedding season to support jewellery demand through November-December, cushioning Madurai 22K counter rates by roughly 1-2% against further slides.

  6. If you waited until 25 Nov

    A INR 15,000 SIP buys 1.303 g today at INR 11,510 / gram vs a projected 1.318 g on 25 Nov at INR 11,380 / gram - a point-estimate saving of ~INR 169 (~0.015 g). The ~1.1% window spread is smaller than a single 1-3% oversold bounce, so waiting carries as much upside risk as downside.

Cross-month note: early December faces a near-fully-priced Fed hike and a possible January follow-up, but with wedding-season demand peaking, central banks buying at a 321 t/quarter pace, and charts deeply oversold, December is not clearly cheaper than this week's window.
