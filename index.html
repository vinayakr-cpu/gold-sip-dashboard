Verdict details

1. Today's Jump

The Madurai 22K rate rose about 1.64% (Rs 225) intraday to Rs 13,965/g as Malabar and Joyalukkas refreshed their 8 Aug rates, an unusually sharp single-day move.

2. Geopolitical Cooldown

Reuters' 28 Jul 2026 analyst poll trimmed the 2026 median gold forecast to $4,509/oz from $4,916/oz as Iran-war tensions eased and the Strait of Hormuz reopened, reducing the safe-haven premium.

3. Rate Pause

RBI held its repo rate at 5.25% and trimmed the FY27 CPI forecast to 5%, a steady backdrop that removes any near-term domestic catalyst for a fresh gold rally.

4. Retailer Lag

Kalyan Jewellers had not refreshed its 22K rate by 11:43 IST on 8 Aug and was still quoting Friday's Rs 13,740/g, suggesting some Madurai counters may briefly lag today's jump.

5. Ample SIP Runway

The SIP deadline of 25 Aug is 17 days away, giving room to watch for a pullback after today's sharp uptick before committing the full Rs 15,000.

Waiting for the recommended day (11 Aug) instead of buying today could save about INR 70 on a Rs 15,000 SIP purchase (~0.005g more gold).

September brings the next Fed meeting and fresh CPI prints; if gold's post-Iran-war retreat continues, early-September rates could undercut late-August levels, but this is not yet confirmed by current data.
