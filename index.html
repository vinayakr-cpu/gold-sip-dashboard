Verdict Details

1. International correction

COMEX gold has fallen to ~$3,874-4,023/oz from a 3-month high of $4,734.60/oz, down ~0.9% over 7 days and ~4.6% over 30 days, dragging Madurai 22K rates down with it.

2. Hawkish Fed stance

The Fed is holding at 3.75%-4.00% after a September hike, with ~90% odds of another hold at the Oct 28 meeting, keeping the dollar and real yields supportive against gold into early November.

3. Retailer rates already falling

Malabar, Joyalukkas and Kalyan all cut 22K rates from Rs13,040/g to Rs12,730/g day-on-day (-2.4%), while Tanishq held flat at Rs13,090/g, confirming the correction is live at the counter.

4. Festive demand floor approaching

Dhanteras (~Nov 6) and Diwali (~Nov 8) fall inside the SIP window, and the World Gold Council expects festive/wedding demand to firm prices once the current pullback plays out.

5. Deadline cushion

With 25 days of runway to the Nov 25 deadline, there is room to wait for the projected Nov 2 dip (~Rs12,700/g) without deadline pressure.

Waiting for the recommended day, 02 Nov, could save ~INR 140/g (~0.0111 g) versus buying today on a INR 15,000 SIP.

Cross-month note: If Fed hawkishness persists past Dhanteras/Diwali, early-December rates could stay soft into the next SIP cycle, but confidence on that read is low given festive-demand crosscurrents.
