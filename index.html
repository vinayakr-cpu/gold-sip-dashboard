Verdict details
1. 1-Year Low
Domestic gold hit its lowest level since October 2025, with MCX Dec futures near Rs 1,38,419 per 10 grams on Nov 2, 2026, after a roughly 3% weekly and 8.15% monthly decline.
2. Festival Demand Ahead
Dhanteras (Nov 6) and Diwali (Nov 8, 2026) are days away; Anand Rathi's Thomas Stephen calls the current correction a good buying opportunity as wedding-season demand typically limits further declines.
3. Fed Hawkish Pressure
CME FedWatch-implied December hike odds are near 80% and the 10-year Treasury yield sits near 4.9-5.0%, capping any sharp international gold rebound.
4. Weak Rupee Cushion
USD/INR near 94.9 keeps the rupee close to recent lows, cushioning the INR gold price even as the dollar gold price corrects, which limits further downside in Madurai rates.
5. Central Bank Floor
Central banks bought a record ~289 tonnes of gold in Q2 2026, providing a structural long-term floor even amid this near-term correction.
If the Fed signals a December pause instead of a hike, gold could extend losses into early December, making a next-month SIP marginally cheaper - but Dhanteras/Diwali demand argues for buying within this window rather than waiting.
