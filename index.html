VERDICT DETAILS

1. Fed hike near-certain

CME data shows a 95.6% probability of a 25bp Dec 9 hike, with Waller open to 50bp, which has already pulled spot gold down toward $4,096/oz intraday.

2. Yields rising

The US 10Y yield jumped to ~4.86-4.88% after a 165K October payrolls beat, raising the opportunity cost of holding gold.

3. Weak India demand

World Gold Council sees 2026 India gold demand at a six-year low (~650 tonnes), down 29% y/y in Q3, reducing local premium pressure.

4. Rupee offset

USD/INR near Rs 93.6-93.9 is keeping some upward pressure on INR gold even as dollar gold softens, which is why Madurai rates are not falling as fast as COMEX.

5. CPI event risk tonight

US October CPI lands this evening IST; a hot core print (0.3% m/m or higher) could trigger a sharper gold sell-off into Nov 11-12 per Waller's stated threshold.

Waiting until 13 Nov could save ~INR 55 / gram (~0.004 g more gold for a INR 15,000 SIP) versus buying today.

If the Fed delivers the priced-in December hike as expected, gold could stay soft into early December, so next month's SIP window may open at similar or slightly lower levels than today.
