VERDICT DETAILS

1. Retail dip today

22K rates pulled back to roughly INR 14,010-14,240/g across major retailers on 17 Sep, a mild retreat from 16 Sep levels (about INR 14,110/g at Tanishq Tamil Nadu).

2. Central bank demand

Central banks bought a record 288.9 tonnes of gold in Q2 2026, underpinning a structural floor under global prices.

3. Festival season approaching

Navratri and Diwali buying from October typically lifts jewellery demand, which can firm retail premiums as the season nears.

4. Rising bond yields

The US 10-year Treasury yield near 5.00-5.01% (up about 0.30 points over the past month) raises the opportunity cost of holding gold, a mild headwind.

5. Rupee under pressure

USD/INR ranged between 95.12 and 96.11 over the past week; a weaker rupee raises the INR cost of imported gold.

Cross-month note: Festival-driven demand could firm prices further into October; there is no clear evidence next month will be cheaper.
