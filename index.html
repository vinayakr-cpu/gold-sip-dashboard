Verdict Details
1. Four-day slide

Retailer board rates are down INR 560/gram (-4.2%) from INR 13,255 (Malabar, 9 Nov) to INR 12,695 today, including a fresh INR 120/gram cut this morning.

2. Rebound underway

Spot gold bounced +0.5% to USD 3,894.52/oz and MCX Dec futures rose 0.40-0.84% to INR 1,36,774-1,37,372/10g on Friday after Thursday's one-year low, so Monday's board rate likely claws back part of today's cut.

3. Rupee risk

USD/INR sits at 94.05 with analysts flagging a slide to 94.50-95.00, and a 1% weaker rupee adds roughly INR 127/gram to Madurai 22K rates.

November wedding demand is drawing buyers back after a INR 72,000-crore festive splurge, with Crisil seeing rising footfalls through December - a local premium tailwind into the 25 Nov deadline.

5. Hawkish Fed caveat

CME FedWatch prices about 89% odds of a 25bp December hike after hot October CPI (US headline +0.4% m/m), which caps rallies and is why confidence is MEDIUM rather than HIGH.

Waiting scenario: the only days projected marginally cheaper than today are 18-20 Nov (~INR 12,700/g, LOW confidence, contingent on a hot US PPI extending the hawkish slide); the expected saving is under INR 10/g (~INR 100 on INR 15,000) against a +INR 50-150/g risk if the bounce extends - not worth waiting for.

Cross-month: next month is not clearly cheaper - the December Fed hike (~89% priced) is largely in the price, while wedding demand and rupee risk near 94.50-95.00 bias December board rates higher; today's one-year-low entry is hard to beat.
